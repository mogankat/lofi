Privacy

Lofi Focus is a pomodoro timer with music, ambient sounds and a journal. It runs entirely in your web browser.

In short: there are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no server of ours that receives your data. Everything you create stays in your browser unless you choose to save it to your own Google Drive or Calendar.

What's stored, and where

Your journal, to-do lists, habits, countdowns, focus history, music stations, sound mix and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on this device. They never leave it unless you download a backup or use the Google features below. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them.

Google Drive and Google Calendar (optional)

These only run after you sign in to Google from the app (Sign in with Google, Connect, Save to Drive or Restore from Drive). Google asks for your permission in its own window.

  • Google Drive (drive.file): the app creates a Lofi Focus folder in your Drive and saves your backup and readable copies of your journal, to-dos and habits there. While Sync across devices is on (it turns on when you sign in, and you can turn it off), it also keeps a sync file there (your journal, habits, to-dos and focus history) and reads it back on your other devices. This permission only lets it see files it created itself, never the rest of your Drive.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): the app reads events from your primary calendar for the month you're viewing, so it can show them in its calendar. If you turn on Add finished focus sessions to Google Calendar, it adds a “🍅 Focus” event when a session ends. Events are held in memory while the page is open and are not stored.
  • Sign-in gives this page a temporary access token (about an hour). It's kept in memory only, never saved, and never sent anywhere except Google.
  • Data received from Google is used only to show it to you and to save your own data back to your own account. It's never shared, sold, used for advertising, or sent to anyone else. This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other services the page talks to

Some features load things from other sites. Those sites see your IP address, as with any web request:

  • YouTube: when you play a YouTube station, the official YouTube player loads, and YouTube's own privacy policy and cookies apply.
  • Radio streams (such as SomaFM): when you play a radio station, audio is streamed from that station.
  • Open-Meteo: if you turn on the temperature, the page sends Open-Meteo either your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) or the city name you type, to get the current weather.
  • Google Fonts: the page's font is loaded from Google Fonts.

Ambient sounds and the animated scenes are generated in your browser and need nothing from the network.

Children

Lofi Focus isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone. It doesn't collect data at all.
